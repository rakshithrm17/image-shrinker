# 🔒 Privacy Policy  
## Product: Image Shrinker  
_Last Updated: 2025_

We care deeply about user privacy.  
Image Shrinker is designed so that your images **never leave your device**.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we handle your images, and your rights as a user.

---

## 1. Data We Collect
### ✔ We DO NOT collect:
- Your images  
- EXIF metadata from images  
- Personal data  
- Location information  
- Uploaded files  
- Browser history  
- Cloud data  
- Device storage content  

### ✔ We DO NOT store:
- Images  
- Logs of images  
- Copies of processed files  
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

### ✔ We DO NOT track:
- What images you compress  
- How many images you compress  
- Content inside the images  

---

## 2. How Images Are Processed
Image Shrinker uses **WebAssembly (WASM)** to compress images **locally inside your browser**.

Meaning:
- Your images are processed **entirely on your device**
- Images are **NOT uploaded** to any server
- Images are **NOT stored** on our servers  
- We **cannot access** your files in any way  
- Once you close or refresh the page, everything is cleared automatically  

Your images remain **100% private**.

---

## 3. Local-Only Processing
All image compression runs on:
- The user’s device memory  
- The browser environment  

We **never** send your images to:
- A server  
- A cloud platform  
- A third-party service  

This ensures:
- No privacy risk  
- No possibility of data theft  
- No accidental logging  

---

## 4. Analytics (Optional)
If enabled in the future (MVP = off), we may collect:
- Anonymous usage counts (e.g., number of visits)
- Browser type (Chrome, Safari, etc.)
- Language and country (not precise location)

Important:
- Analytics will **never** include images or private content.
- No personal identifiers (name, email, phone, image content).
- You may opt out at any time.

---

## 5. Cookies
Currently, Image Shrinker:
- Does **NOT** use cookies  
- Does **NOT** store any data in the browser local storage  

In future updates:
- We may store UI preferences (dark mode, quality settings)  
- These will remain **device-only**, not sent to any server  

---

## 6. Third-Party Services
We do **not** use:
- Cloud storage  
- External image processors  
- AI models that upload content  
- File-sharing platforms  

If we add optional analytics later:
- Analytics tools will not receive images  
- Only basic usage metrics will be shared  
- No personal identifying data  

---

## 7. Data Security
Because images are **never transmitted**, risks are extremely low.

Security model includes:
- All processing on user device  
- No backend endpoints  
- No stored files  
- No central database  

This is one of the safest architectures possible.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy
Since we do not collect any personal data:
- The service is safe for users of all ages  
- No special child data is collected  

We still recommend supervision for minors under 13 if they handle sensitive photos.

Updates will always:

The last updated date will change accordingly.
