# 👤 User Rights & Responsibilities  
## Product: Image Shrinker  
_Last Updated: 2025_

This document describes the rights and responsibilities of users who access and use Image Shrinker.  
It ensures transparency, trust, and a safe experience for everyone.

---

# 1. User Rights

## ✔ 1.1 Right to Privacy
Users have the right to complete privacy because:
- Images stay entirely on their own device  
- No image files are uploaded, stored, or logged  
- No personal data is collected  

Users may use the tool knowing their photos never leave their browser.

---

## ✔ 1.2 Right to Use the Service Freely
Users may:
- Upload and compress images they own  
- Download compressed versions  
- Use the compressed images for personal, academic, or professional use  
- Share compressed images with others  

No registration or account is required.

---

## ✔ 1.3 Right to Transparency
Users have the right to know:
- How their images are processed  
- That Image Shrinker operates client-side  
- That no backend or cloud processing occurs  
- What data (if any) the app collects (currently none)

All details are clearly explained in the Privacy Policy.

---

## ✔ 1.4 Right to Safety & Security
Users have the right to:
- A secure, encrypted browser environment  
- Zero external access to their stored photos  
- No unauthorized sharing of their files  

---

## ✔ 1.5 Right to Stop Using the Service Anytime
Users may close the app at any time.  
No data remains stored after closing the tab or refreshing.

---

# 2. User Responsibilities

## ❗ 2.1 Responsibility for Uploaded Content
Users must:
- Upload only images they legally own  
- Not upload copyrighted material without permission  
- Not upload illegal, harmful, or inappropriate images  

Users are entirely responsible for the content they process.

---

## ❗ 2.2 Responsibility for Output Usage
Users must ensure:
- The compressed output meets required quality standards  
- The output format is supported by the platforms they use  
- They review images before using them in official documents

Image Shrinker is not liable for incorrect usage of compressed files.

---

## ❗ 2.3 Respect for Application Integrity
Users must not:
- Try to reverse-engineer the compression engine  
- Attempt to exploit, hack, or damage the service  
- Upload content with malicious intent  
- Abuse the system with automated or excessive requests  

---

## ❗ 2.4 Understanding Limitations
Users accept that:
- Compression results may vary by image  
- Extremely fine details may change slightly in some modes  
- Browser/device limitations may affect performance  

---

# 3. Shared Responsibilities

Both the user and Image Shrinker must work to:
- Maintain a safe and respectful environment  
- Prevent misuse  
- Protect privacy  

---

# 4. Summary
Users have the right to:
- Privacy  
- Safe usage  
- Free access  
- Transparency  

Users are responsible for:
- Using legal and safe images  
- Ensuring output meets their needs  
- Using the service respectfully  

These rights and responsibilities help maintain a high-trust, privacy-focused product experience.
